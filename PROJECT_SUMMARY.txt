❓ QUICK ANSWERS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Can I edit the website?
A: Yes! Open index.html in any text editor. All CSS/JS is embedded.
   Re-save and refresh browser to see changes.

Q: How do I add my content?
A: Edit the text directly in the HTML. Look for sections like:
   - "VISUAL ENGINEERING" (tagline)
   - Service card titles and descriptions
   - Navigation menu items

Q: Can I change colors?
A: Yes! Search for color codes in the CSS section:
   - #000000 (black) → change to your color
   - #FFFFFF (white) → change to your color
   - #DC143C (red) → change to your color

Q: Will gyroscope work on my phone?
A: Yes, on real iOS 13+ and most Android devices.
   NOT on desktop browsers (mouse parallax instead).
   NOT in mobile browser simulators (test on real device).

Q: How do I track analytics?
A: Add Google Analytics ID to the HTML.
   Or use Netlify/Vercel built-in analytics.

Q: Can I make it faster?
A: Already optimized! 60fps, 158KB file, ~2s load.
   Further optimization requires compression/CDN (overkill for most).
